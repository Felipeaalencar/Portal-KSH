Privacy Policy

Kilian Smart Homes LLC · Last updated: May 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Kilian Smart Homes LLC ("we", "us", "our") operates Portal KSH, an internal business operations platform used by our team to manage client service orders, customer relationships, tool inventory, and financial reporting. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to operate our smart home services business:

  • Client information: name, address, phone, email, equipment installed, and service history
  • Employee information: names, roles, work hours, and service activities
  • Financial information: invoices, payments, expenses, profit/loss data (accessed via QuickBooks integration)
  • Technical data: browser type, IP address, and usage logs for security and analytics

3. How We Use Information

  • Deliver smart home installation and maintenance services
  • Schedule technicians and track service orders
  • Generate invoices and process payments
  • Maintain client maintenance schedules
  • Comply with tax and accounting obligations
  • Improve operational efficiency

4. Third-Party Services

Portal KSH integrates with the following third-party services. Their respective privacy policies apply to data they process:

  • Intuit QuickBooks — financial data and accounting
  • Supabase — database hosting
  • Vercel — application hosting
  • Google Maps — address visualization

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data. We may share data only with:

  • Service providers listed above, as necessary to operate Portal KSH
  • Legal authorities when required by applicable law
  • Tax and accounting professionals supporting our compliance

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS), authenticated access, role-based permissions, and limited data retention. While we strive to protect personal information, no system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccuracies in your data
  • Request deletion (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Object to certain processing of your data

8. Children's Privacy

Portal KSH is an internal business tool not intended for use by individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

9. International Data Transfers

Our service providers may store data in the United States or other countries. By using Portal KSH, you acknowledge that your data may be transferred and processed in jurisdictions outside your country of residence.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be noted with a revised "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
